# Learning by Design review framework

## Knowledge processes

Look for purposeful opportunities to:

- **Experience**: engage the known or new through situated, meaningful
  materials, practices, or phenomena.
- **Conceptualize**: name, classify, generalize, and develop a metalanguage.
- **Analyze**: examine relationships, functions, assumptions, perspectives,
  causes, consequences, or evidence.
- **Apply**: use and redesign knowledge in a new, consequential, or
  context-sensitive setting.

These are not a compulsory four-step lesson script. A strong review identifies
which processes are present, which are deliberately deferred, and whether the
overall sequence supports the stated outcome.

## Assessment lens

Prefer evidence that is:

- aligned to explicit, criterion-referenced criteria;
- embedded in meaningful knowledge activity;
- capable of showing process, growth, transfer, collaboration, and judgment
  when those are outcomes; and
- usable for feedback and revision, not solely a terminal score.

This does not prohibit short tests. Ask what evidence a test can support and
what additional evidence is needed for outcomes it cannot represent.
