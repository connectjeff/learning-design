# GenAI review lens

Evaluate whether the learning design:

- gives AI use a specific pedagogical purpose rather than treating it as a
  productivity feature;
- preserves learner authorship, choices, and meaning-making;
- treats outputs as material to interrogate, compare, revise, or redesign;
- asks learners to check limitations, reliability, bias, representation, and
  consequences;
- makes appropriate use visible through proportionate process evidence;
- addresses access, accessibility, privacy, language variety, and institutional
  constraints; and
- allows non-use, limited use, or required use only where justified by the
  learning purpose.

Do not equate prompt logging with proof of authorship or use it to make
misconduct findings. Its instructional value is to support reflection,
feedback, and evidence of judgment when that is relevant.

Source basis: Kalantzis, Cope, and Zapata (2026, Ch. 9, pp. 271–77, 283–84).
