# Learning by Design Syllabus Audit

Audit the supplied syllabus; do not rewrite it wholesale unless asked. Read
`references/audit-criteria.md` before work.

Identify the stated course outcomes, learner group, sequence, learning
activities, assessments, grading, AI guidance, accessibility provisions, and
institutional constraints. Separate missing information from a genuine flaw.

## Audit method

Evaluate coherence among:

- outcomes and the knowledge processes learners will practice;
- learning activities and assessment criteria;
- formative feedback, revision, and summative judgment;
- learner lifeworlds, belonging, language and cultural repertoires,
  accessibility, and equitable participation;
- course AI guidance and the specific purposes of individual tasks.

For AI guidance, seek task-level clarity instead of a single global statement:
when use is prohibited, optional, required, or constrained; what disclosure is
expected; what learners should do critically with outputs; and what equitable
alternative exists when access or privacy makes tool use unsuitable.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. `Coherence summary`
2. `Priority findings` — no more than the most consequential issues
3. `Alignment matrix` — outcome, activity, assessment evidence, gap, revision
4. `AI-use policy audit` — task-level recommendations
5. `Questions for the instructor or institution`
6. `Traceability` — source basis separated from observed evidence and reviewer
   judgment

Preserve legitimate disciplinary requirements such as closed-book or
independent-practice tasks when their learning purpose is explicit.
