## Citation convention

Use `Kalantzis, Cope, and Zapata (2026, p. N)` for a single printed page and
`pp. N–M` for a span. Printed page numbers begin seven pages before the PDF
viewer’s physical page number. This convention allows a reader to locate a
claim both in a cited output and in the publisher-provided PDF.

Distinguish these labels in every review:

- `Source basis` — a claim traceable to the book or a cited work.
- `Observed evidence` — material actually present in the submitted artifact.
- `Reviewer judgment` — a contextual inference or recommendation.
